# House of Jars photo library

The house's own photos and brand files, taken by the House of Jars team in Laos (the team confirmed this on 4 October 2026). These are the originals, kept as they arrived. When a photo is used on the website, an optimised copy goes into `public/photos/`, so nothing here is served to visitors.

**Names:** lowercase words joined by hyphens, saying what the photo shows. `-branded` means the "House of Jars" name and logo are printed on the photo; for the website a clean version without them is better.

**Still to come:** the café and ground floor (evening photos), the bathrooms and showers, the outside in daylight, and the area.
