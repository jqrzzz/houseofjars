Opening the walk…

Accurate and simple: three rules

  1. Count exactly. Beds, lockers, toilets, extinguishers. Not sure? Leave it as ?
  2. Place roughly. Tap where each area sits on its floor. No tape measure.
  3. Photograph everything. One photo from a corner of each area is plenty. Keep guests and their things out of shot.

Areas tagged Guess are what Claude worked out from your photos, signs and the Booking.com listing. Fix anything wrong, then tap Mark as checked. When you've walked every floor, tell Claude in the chat.
