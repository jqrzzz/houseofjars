POST CAPTION (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp)

Three house rules for a calm night.

1. No shoes upstairs: wear them on the ground floor, then take them off at the foot of the stairs before you go up. Clean floors where everyone sleeps, as in most Lao homes.
2. No smoking anywhere in the house. Smoke out past the terrace, by the small jar for cigarette butts, not on the terrace itself. Smoke from the terrace drifts straight into the café.
3. Quiet hours 21:00–07:00. Keep your voice down at all times. Most guests come here to sleep well.
